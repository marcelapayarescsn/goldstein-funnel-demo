Content Pipeline Map · Prepared by C-Suite Network

From a raw asset to seven output lanes.

Here's how every piece of Rich's content moves: it comes in as a raw recording, passes through three quality filters, and from there gets routed into seven output lanes — articles, infographics, media pitches, boardroom scripts, boards, the thought council, and the Hero Club. Click any lane to see the detail: inputs, outputs, and current status.

STAGE 1

Raw assets

  • Podcast episodes (Innovations & Breakthroughs)
  • Patent Question videos
  • Goldstein Patent Law videos
  • Rich's own decks and documents (Sales Deck, workshop materials)
STAGE 2

Transcribe and verify

  • Every source is transcribed in full before anything is selected.
  • No timestamp or quote is used from memory.
  • Every claim is checked against the actual transcript.
STAGE 3

Quality gate · two rules

  • Rule 1 — Rich only. Guest-dominated episodes are excluded, even if the guest's story is compelling.
  • Rule 2 — Self-contained and short. One hook plus one payoff, 15 to 90 seconds, understandable with no added context.
STAGE 4

Approve before cutting

  • The full list (source, timestamp, exact quote) is reviewed before a single clip is cut.
  • Nothing goes into the Snippets folder without sign off first.

Seven output lanes
